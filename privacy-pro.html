Privacy Policy — ScanVault Pro

Developer: SL Apps · Last updated: November 5, 2026

ScanVault Pro (the “App”) is a barcode scanning and inventory management application. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party. The App contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and it does not request the internet permission.

Data Storage

All data created or imported while using the App — scanned barcodes, groups, inventory folders, imported files and exported reports — is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

Permissions Used

  • Camera: used only to scan barcodes/QR codes in real time. Camera frames are processed on-device and are never recorded, stored or transmitted.
  • Storage / Files: used to import files you select and to save the export files you choose to create and share.
  • Vibration: used for scan feedback.

Sharing Exported Files

When you use “Export” or “Share”, the file is handed to the operating system’s share sheet. Where you send it next is your choice and is governed by that destination’s own privacy policy.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new “Last updated” date.
