Privacy Policy — ScanVault Scanner (Free)

Developer: SL Apps · Last updated: November 5, 2026

ScanVault Scanner (the “App”) is a barcode scanning and inventory management application. This policy explains how the free version of the App handles information.

Your scan data stays on your device

All data you create or import — scanned barcodes, groups, inventory folders, imported Excel/CSV files and exported reports — is stored locally on your device only. The developer does not receive, collect or store it. Uninstalling the App removes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows advertising provided by Google AdMob:

  • a banner on the Home and Groups screens, and
  • a short rewarded video before you export or share in a format other than TXT.

To show ads, Google AdMob may collect and use, on its own behalf, data such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data. The App does not give AdMob your scanned data, files or inventory. How Google uses this information is described in Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area), the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are used. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Internet: the free version needs an internet connection only to show ads. Scanning and TXT export work without internet. Exports in other formats require an internet connection because of the ad.

Want no ads and no internet permission? The paid ScanVault Pro version contains no advertising.

Permissions Used

  • Camera: used only to scan barcodes/QR codes in real time. Camera frames are processed on-device and are never recorded, stored or transmitted.
  • Storage / Files: used to import files you select and to save the export files you choose to create and share.
  • Internet / network state: used only to load advertising.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for advertising.
  • Vibration: used for scan feedback.

Sharing Exported Files

When you use “Export” or “Share”, the file is handed to the operating system’s share sheet. Where you send it next is your choice and is governed by that destination’s own privacy policy.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new “Last updated” date.
